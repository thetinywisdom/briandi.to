# Cover art

Art from [SteamGridDB](https://www.steamgriddb.com), made by its community, saved as web copies. Each game is shown in its platform's case on the GAME page: posters (2:3) for the tall cases (PSP, PS2, PS4, PS5, PC), square grids for the PS1 jewel case and the Game Boy and Super Nintendo boxes.

The cases are pictures, rendered ahead of time into `cases/` by `tools/render-cases.cjs`. To change a cover, replace its file here and re-render (see the comment above the games grid in `index.html`). Art that does not fit the case's shape is cropped, keeping the middle, or the part named by `data-align="top"` / `"bottom"` on its case.
