# Plan

Status of the briandi.to project, for whoever picks it up next. It spans three repositories:

- `thetinywisdom/briandi.to` — this one, the static site
- `thetinywisdom/retro-3d-assets` — the 3D web components the site is built on
- `thetinywisdom/briandi.to-channels` — what plays on the channel TV (`channels.json` and `media/`); public, read by the site at run time

Last updated: 2026-11-12.

## Where things stand

The site works end to end with placeholder content. It is being put on GitHub Pages at https://thetinywisdom.github.io/briandi.to/ (see Next up). The owner follows progress through a preview link (see Working notes).

**Current look (decided by the owner):** a near-black room. Four Sony CRTs stand on a moss green carpet, lit only by their own screens, with a dim green light behind them, still haze (static smoke), still film grain and a faint front lamp so the buttons can be seen. The sets stand in one tower on every screen (on wide screens they used to be in two stacks): from the top the small portable (channel TV), the orange TV, the 9-inch monitor and the 20-inch.

**Current interaction (decided by the owner):** tap a TV to move in to it; tap its screen to open that TV's page full screen as a normal scrolling page; `Esc` returns. The goal is that content is easy to read. This holds for TVs 1–3 (20-inch home, 9-inch latest strip, orange about); TV 4 is the channel TV below.

**Channel TV (decided by the owner, 2026-11-12):** TV 4, the KV-5200 portable on top of the orange set, is a channel TV, not a page. It never opens full screen and stays at TV zoom (`no-reader="kv-5200"`). It has 14 channels (1–12, U1, U2; renumbered from the real set's 2–13 at the owner's request, 2026-11-12) switched by its own keys; each channel plays one piece of media: a YouTube link, a Spotify link, or an uploaded GIF / WebP. The channel content lives in its own repository, `briandi.to-channels` (owner's choice, 2026-11-12), which the site fetches from GitHub at run time, so the owner can change channels without touching this repository. Channel 2 is "How to use" (owner's copy, 2026-11-12; on 2026-11-13 the three slides about the other TVs became one, at the owner's request: "The other TVs:" with CREATE / GAME / WORK and a few words each, in stack order): seven lines in the guide's style, one at a time, auto-advancing at reading speed, `< PREV  01 / 09 ■□□  NEXT >` pagination (owner's request), restarting at line 1 on tuning in; built into the site, so `channels.json` starts at channel 3. Channel 1 is the "Program Guide" (owner's title), a green-phosphor channel list (styled after two references the owner supplied: a radar "BUNKER" screen and a "NUE 2020" menu) built from the channels that are on; each line is a button that tunes the set (owner's request: visitors expect to click them). The set's own channel keys are lit like a backlit keyboard all the time (owner's words; at first only while the guide was on): a soft glow round each key and the number cells faintly lit, fading in and then breathing between 10% and 20% brightness on an eased 4 s pulse (owner's request; first 10–30% over 2.4 s), with a green ring rippling out from key 2 every 2 s the first time the guide is on after a page load (gone for good once the visitor changes channel or leaves the TV; back after a refresh) (a drawn tapping hand was tried and removed: the owner found it looked like a rude gesture) (an HTML overlay placed on the key with `shelf.controlPoint()`) (`setBacklight()`; a glow plane behind each KV-5200 channel key, not on the real set). Earlier attempts (a shimmer on the on-screen list, then a wave over the keys) were not what the owner meant. Empty channels show static. Its pager key is "TV". Sound (owner's decision, 2026-11-12): YouTube channels start with sound when tuned in, follow the TV's volume slider and the pager's Sound key, and if a browser blocks the sound they play muted and unmute at the visitor's next click. Spotify cannot be volume-controlled from the page; visitors press its own play button. The channel TV only plays while the camera is on it (owner's request): moving to another TV or back to the room pauses YouTube and songs where they are (Spotify is unloaded) and they carry on when the visitor comes back. The same goes for changing channel: a YouTube player loads the first time its channel is tuned, then pauses off air and resumes from the same point (owner's request). Songs the page plays itself (channel 5) go through an old-TV speaker sound (owner's request): bass cut below 250 Hz, top softened above 3.5 kHz, a boxy lift at 1.2 kHz; YouTube and Spotify play inside their own players, so the page cannot change their sound. Now playing (owner's decision, 2026-11-12): while the camera is on the channel TV and it is on a filled channel, the pager's LCD shows "CH 04 NOW PLAYING", the kind (VIDEO / MUSIC / GIF) and the title: the real video or track title for YouTube (reported by the player) and Spotify (from Spotify's oEmbed), the file name for a GIF / WebP. Empty channels read "No signal on this channel". Picture lists (`images`) play each GIF / WebP through once, then the next, forever (owner's request: once, not repeated); only the picture on screen and the next one are downloaded, and only while the channel is on. Pictures fill the screen at their own aspect ratio (`object-fit: cover`, owner's request): never stretched, so the sides of a 16:9 GIF are cut off.

**Navigation (decided by the owner, 2026-11-12):** all navigation is one bottom dock, a 3D pager (`<retro-pager>`, design "A" of five 2D mockups). Its LCD always shows something: what the pointed-at pager key is (only the pager's own keys change it; following the mouse over the TVs was removed at the owner's request, it looked like a glitch), or else the scene's hint ("Tap a TV to look closer"). Keys: one per TV, which always take you out to the room, in on that TV: pressing the key of the TV you are on does nothing, and pressing any TV key while a page is open full screen closes it (owner, 2026-11-13; before, a second press opened the page full screen and keys switched pages while reading). Only tapping a TV's screen opens its page, Back, Sound, Info. The scene's own hint, Back and Read buttons are hidden by the site's CSS. Look, as revised by the owner: plenty of space round the LCD, body corners about as round as the LCD bezel (not a pill), LCD lettered in DotGothic16, long LCD text resting 2 s at its start, then scrolling round (owner first asked for no delay, then for 2 s so the start can be read). The pager's own key numbers read "TV 01–04"; the channel TV's channel shows as "CH 03" etc.

## Known gaps

- **The preview artifact cannot show YouTube or Spotify** (its sandbox blocks other sites in frames) and cannot fetch from GitHub; the preview build publishes `channels.json` and `media/` next to the page instead, so GIF / WebP channels do show there. The real site can show all of them.
- **Pager on phones covers the bottom of the tower** (the big TV's lower edge). The dock text on the Info key and the TV keys' descriptions are placeholders.
- **The pager is a generic shape**, not modelled on a real pager.
- **Not tested on a real phone or with real touch.** Everything was checked in headless desktop Chromium at desktop and phone viewport sizes.
- **Performance is unmeasured.** The scene now only redraws on interaction (the smoke is static). Low-end phone behaviour is unknown.
- **The tube page is not clickable on TVs 1–3.** A tap on a focused screen opens the reader, so links only work in the full-screen view. This was a deliberate trade for readability. The channel TV is the exception: its screen takes clicks once zoomed in.
- **No real YouTube or Spotify link has been tried yet.** Channel switching, unloading of inactive players and the clickable screen were tested with a test picture, a local test page and a stand-in `channels.json`; reading the real (empty) list from GitHub was tested.
- **YouTube sound control was tested against a stand-in player**, because this environment cannot reach YouTube. Safari's behaviour (most likely to block sound) is untested.
- **Not tested in Safari / WebKit** (not installed in the agent's environment). Channel 5's scroll was a tall CSS-animated layer that stayed black on the owner's iPhone; it is now drawn on a 640 × 480 canvas, checked in Chromium only.
- **The site depends on raw.githubusercontent.com for channels.** If GitHub is down or the repository goes private, the guide shows every channel as NO SIGNAL and the rest of the site works.
- **Tower on phones is taller than the screen**, so the big TV starts partly below the fold and the visitor scrolls to it.
- **PVM-20M4E front panel and KV-9PR1 top buttons are reconstructions**, not measured. A straight-on photo of each would allow correcting them.
- **Case and book designs are from memory**, not matched to photos. Platform names are plain lettering, not real logos.
- **Handheld screens** (only visible with `consoles`) are plain glowing colours.
- **Untested paths:** cover images on shelf items, the Open button on item cards, a logo image as the maker's badge, the React wrapper, keyboard-only use, sounds.
- **No automated tests** in either repository.

## Working notes

- The bundle in `vendor/retro-3d.js` must be rebuilt and copied from `retro-3d-assets` after any 3D change; see `CLAUDE.md` in each repository.
- **Always publish a preview after every change (owner's standing request, 2026-11-12).** The owner previews work through a single-file build of the site published as an artifact (the owner has shared it as "anyone with the link"), updated in place at the same link: https://claude.ai/artifact/Qyfjne9njFyZAonph82WGT (pass it as `url` when publishing from a new session, after reading it). To build it from `index.html`: replace the `styles.css` link with a `<style>` holding the file; set `const BUNDLE` in the loading-screen script to `null`; add one `<script type="module">` holding the bundle (with `</script` escaped as `<\/script`) where `</body>` was; remove the doctype, `<html>`, `<head>`, `<body>` tags and the charset and viewport metas, which the publish wrapper adds; set `const CHANNELS` to `'channels/'` and publish `briandi.to-channels`' `channels.json` and `media/*` with the page as `files` (`channels/channels.json`, `channels/media/...`). Test it locally with a `<meta charset="utf-8">` in front, or the bundle's Japanese regular expressions fail to parse.
- **Merge to `main` when work is done** (owner's request, 2026-11-12). Sessions may develop on a `claude/…` branch; fast-forward `main` to it and push both repositories.
- The cloud environment's allowed hosts include `www.adcom.it` and `pro.sony`, added early on to fetch two PDFs. Both sites refused automated downloads, so the entries are unused and can be removed.
